About

Hello, I'm Amber Hamersfeld, a designer based in Melbourne working across brand identity, packaging, and editorial design. My practice sits at the intersection of strategy and craft. I'm drawn to projects that need clear thinking, a strong point of view, and a considered visual language that earns its place on the shelf or on the screen. Recent work spans health and wellness, food and hospitality, and independent publishing. I'm currently open to new projects, freelance briefs, and collaborations.
