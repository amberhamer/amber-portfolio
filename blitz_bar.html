Blitz Bar Mural

A bold mural artwork created for Blitz Bar, designed to bring movement, energy and personality into the venue. The piece works as both an interior feature and a branded photo moment, extending the venue's atmosphere into the customer experience.

Role
  • Mural Design
  • Illustration
  • Art Direction
  • Environmental Graphics
Deliverables
  • Large-scale mural artwork
  • Interior feature concept
  • Venue photography moments
  • Applied wall graphics
Year

2023

Design challenge

Create an artwork that could hold its own at architectural scale while supporting the venue's social, energetic and hospitality-led experience.

Creative direction

High-impact, graphic and playful. The mural uses strong shapes and confident colour to create a lively focal point that feels immediate from across the room and detailed up close.

Outcome

A finished environmental artwork that adds visual identity to the bar interior and gives the space a memorable branded moment.
